**Main Data Integrity Risks**

1. **Human Error**: Human errors pose a significant risk to data integrity, including manual transcription errors, missing audit trails, uncontrolled spreadsheets, delayed documentation, inconsistent naming conventions, and lack of validation checks.
2. **Technological Barriers**: Legacy systems may lack the necessary capabilities for effective data governance and management, leading to inefficiencies and increased risks of data integrity issues.
3. **Regulatory Compliance Challenges**: Pharmaceutical companies face numerous challenges in maintaining data integrity, including inadequate training, lack of standardized processes for data handling, and complexity of the pharmaceutical industry.
4. **Organizational Culture Pressures**: Production pressure can incentivize result manipulation, not always through deliberate fraud, but through normalized shortcuts, such as backdating entries, selecting favorable test results, or delaying documentation.

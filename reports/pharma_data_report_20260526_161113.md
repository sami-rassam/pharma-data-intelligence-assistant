**Detailed Report: Power BI Dashboard for Deviation Monitoring in GMP Environment**

**Introduction**

In a Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) environment, deviation monitoring is crucial to ensure product quality and safety. A Power BI dashboard can support deviation monitoring by providing real-time visibility into manufacturing processes, enabling prompt response to deviations, and facilitating compliance with regulatory requirements.

**Key Features of a Power BI Dashboard for Deviation Monitoring**

1. **Real-time Data Integration**: Integrate data from various sources, such as Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES), Laboratory Information Management Systems (LIMS), and Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems, to provide a comprehensive view of manufacturing processes.
2. **Deviation Detection**: Use machine learning algorithms to detect deviations in real-time, enabling prompt response to potential quality issues.
3. **Alert System**: Implement an alert system to notify stakeholders of deviations, ensuring timely action is taken to prevent product quality issues.
4. **CAPA Tracking**: Automate CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Action) tracking to ensure that deviations are properly addressed and preventive measures are implemented.
5. **Audit Readiness**: Generate voluminous data logs to support audits, reducing manual audit preparation and ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements.

**Data Integrity Risks in Pharmaceutical Reporting Dashboards**

**Executive Summary**

Data integrity is a critical aspect of pharmaceutical reporting dashboards, ensuring the accuracy, completeness, and reliability of data. This report identifies the main data integrity risks in pharmaceutical reporting dashboards, highlighting the importance of robust systems and practices to maintain data integrity.

**Main Data Integrity Risks**

1. **Manual Transcription Errors**: Human error during data entry can lead to inaccuracies and inconsistencies in data.
2. **Missing Audit Trails**: Inadequate audit trails can compromise the authenticity of records and make it difficult to track changes.
3. **Uncontrolled Spreadsheets**: Spreadsheets can be prone to errors and inconsistencies, and may not be suitable for critical business information.
4. **Delayed Documentation**: Delayed documentation can lead to inaccuracies and inconsistencies in data.
5. **Inconsistent Naming Conventions**: Inconsistent naming conventions can make it difficult to identify and track data.
6. **Lack of Validation Checks**: Inadequate validation checks can compromise the accuracy and reliability of data.
7. **Technological Barriers**: Legacy systems and inadequate training can hinder the ability to effectively manage data integrity.
8. **Human Error**: Human error during data entry, inadequate training, and lack of standardized processes can contribute to data integrity issues.

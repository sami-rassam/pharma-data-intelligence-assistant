What are the main data integrity risks in pharmaceutical reporting dashboards?

## Final Answer

**Final Answer: Main Data Integrity Risks in Pharmaceutical Reporting Dashboards**

**Executive Summary**

Data integrity is a critical component of pharmaceutical reporting dashboards, ensuring the accuracy, completeness, and reliability of critical business information. This report identifies the main data integrity risks in pharmaceutical reporting dashboards, highlighting the importance of robust systems and practices to maintain data integrity.

**Main Data Integrity Risks**

1. **Manual Transcription Errors**: Human errors during data entry can lead to inaccuracies and inconsistencies in reporting dashboards.
2. **Missing Audit Trails**: Inadequate audit trails can compromise the integrity of data, making it difficult to track changes and identify potential issues.
3. **Uncontrolled Spreadsheets**: Spreadsheets can be prone to errors and inconsistencies, particularly if not properly validated and controlled.
4. **Delayed Documentation**: Delayed documentation can lead to inaccuracies and inconsistencies in reporting dashboards.
5. **Inconsistent Naming Conventions**: Inconsistent naming conventions can lead to confusion and errors in reporting dashboards.
6. **Lack of Validation Checks**: Inadequate validation checks can compromise the integrity of data, making it difficult to ensure accuracy and completeness.
7. **Inadequate System Validation**: Inadequate system validation can lead to structural vulnerabilities that create conditions where data can be altered, lost, or misrepresented.
8. **Production Pressure**: Production pressure can incentivize result manipulation, leading to normalized shortcuts such as backdating entries, selecting favorable test results, or delaying documentation.

**Final Answer**

**Detailed Report: Power BI Dashboards Supporting GMP Deviation Monitoring**

**Executive Summary**

Power BI dashboards can significantly support Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) deviation monitoring by providing real-time visibility into deviation trends, root cause analysis, and corrective actions. By leveraging Power BI's data visualization capabilities, pharmaceutical companies can create interactive dashboards that facilitate informed decision-making and improve compliance with regulatory requirements.

**Key Findings**

1.  **Deviation Monitoring Dashboards**: Power BI can be used to create dashboards that track deviation trends, including deviation type breakdown, severity classification, overdue deviations, and repeat deviations.
2.  **Root Cause Analysis**: Power BI's data visualization capabilities can help identify root causes of deviations by analyzing data from various sources, including quality control, production, and environmental monitoring systems.
3.  **Corrective Actions**: Power BI can be used to track corrective actions, including CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Action) closure time, CAPA creation vs. closure trend, and root cause categories.
4.  **Real-time Visibility**: Power BI provides real-time visibility into deviation trends, enabling pharmaceutical companies to respond promptly to deviations and prevent repeat occurrences.
5.  **Compliance**: Power BI dashboards can help pharmaceutical companies demonstrate compliance with regulatory requirements, including 21 CFR Part 11 and EU GMP Annex 11.

**Limitations**

While Power BI dashboards can significantly support GMP deviation monitoring, there are limitations to consider:

*   **Data quality and integrity**: Poor data quality can lead to inaccurate insights and poor decision-making.
*   **Scalability and integration**: Power BI dashboards may require significant resources to integrate with existing systems and data sources.
*   **Regulatory compliance**: Pharmaceutical companies must ensure that Power BI dashboards are aligned with regulatory requirements and industry best practices.
*   **User training and support**: Users may require specialized training to effectively use Power BI dashboards.

STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Title: Batch Record Review Process
Document Number: SOP-QA-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01/05/2026
Department: Quality Assurance

1. PURPOSE
To define the procedure for reviewing completed batch manufacturing records to ensure compliance with GMP requirements and internal quality standards.

2. SCOPE
This procedure applies to all commercial manufacturing batches produced at the facility.

3. RESPONSIBILITIES
QA Officers are responsible for reviewing batch records.
Production Supervisors are responsible for ensuring all manufacturing documentation is complete.
The QA Manager is responsible for final batch disposition approval.

4. PROCEDURE

4.1 Batch Documentation Review
- Verify all manufacturing steps have been completed.
- Confirm all entries are legible and signed.
- Review equipment logbooks for completeness.
- Ensure deviations are documented appropriately.

4.2 Critical Process Verification
- Verify temperature and pressure ranges were maintained.
- Confirm all in-process checks meet specifications.
- Review yield calculations and reconciliation.

4.3 Data Integrity Checks
- Verify audit trails where applicable.
- Ensure no unexplained overwritten entries exist.
- Confirm compliance with ALCOA principles.

4.4 Deviations
- Any discrepancies identified during review must be documented.
- Major deviations require escalation to QA management.
- CAPA actions must be assigned where necessary.

5. REFERENCES
- EU GMP Annex 11
- FDA Data Integrity Guidance
- Internal Deviation Management SOP

6. ABBREVIATIONS
GMP = Good Manufacturing Practice
CAPA = Corrective and Preventive Action
QA = Quality Assurance

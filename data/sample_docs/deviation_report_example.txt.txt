DEVIATION REPORT

Deviation ID: DEV-2026-014
Date Opened: 12/05/2026
Department: Manufacturing

Description:
During batch manufacturing of Product A123, the granulation temperature exceeded the validated upper limit for approximately 12 minutes.

Immediate Actions Taken:
- Process paused
- QA notified immediately
- Impact assessment initiated

Preliminary Investigation:
Initial review suggests a sensor calibration drift may have contributed to inaccurate temperature monitoring.

Risk Assessment:
Potential impact to product quality is considered low pending laboratory analysis.

Corrective Actions:
- Recalibrate temperature sensor
- Review maintenance records
- Retrain operators on escalation procedures

Preventive Actions:
- Increase calibration frequency
- Implement automated temperature alerts
- Add secondary verification checks

Status:
Open – Investigation Ongoing
